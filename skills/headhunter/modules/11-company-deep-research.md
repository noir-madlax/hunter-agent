# Company / Department Deep Research

Use this module before sourcing and whenever a new company, person, partner, competitor, or similar company appears.

## Goal

Turn company and department context into hiring judgment:

- Who owns the business problem?
- Which GM / +1 / +2 / HR stakeholders shape the hiring decision?
- What business motion does the role support?
- Which companies and candidates are truly similar?
- What should the consultant verify before shortlisting?

## Evidence Layers

Keep these layers separate in every output:

| Layer | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 客户提供 | Information directly provided by the client; useful but still needs boundary confirmation. |
| 已公开证实 | Publicly supported by official site, annual/interim report, PR, news, conference, or partner announcement. |
| 人才库佐证 | Supported by internal candidate records, interview notes, candidate feedback, compensation/motivation notes, or organization observations. |
| AI推断 | Reasonable inference from known facts; never present as fact. |
| 待确认 | Material point that needs client, public, or talent database confirmation. |

## Trigger Rule

Every time a new node appears, scan both public information and the talent database:

- Person: GM, business leader, +1, +2, HR leader, interviewer, partner executive.
- Company: client entity, BU, subsidiary, partner, acquisition target, competitor, similar business.
- Team: dealer team, professional solutions, retail, channel, regional sales, clinic network.
- Market signal: acquisition, restructuring, new GM, strategic partnership, expansion, layoffs, integration.

Do not treat same-name talent database records as evidence unless company, title, timeline, or note context matches.

## Company Similarity

Do not compare companies by industry name alone. Compare:

- business model
- channel structure
- customer type
- sales organization
- decision maker
- regulated or clinical environment
- business stage
- recent organization change

For dealer / channel / medical ecosystem HRBP roles, strong similarity usually comes from:

- dealer / distributor / channel sales support
- hospital / pharmacy / clinic / medical device / ophthalmology ecosystem
- regional sales teams with incentive, performance, ER, and productivity issues
- new GM, reorg, acquisition integration, or business transformation

## Candidate Implications

Deep research must update the candidate persona and scoring model. For dealer / professional solutions / medical ecosystem roles, prioritize:

- 经销商 / 渠道 / 区域销售支持
- 医疗 / 眼科 / 医疗器械 / 诊所 / 药房生态相似度
- 新 GM / 新业务负责人 / 组织调整经验
- 销售绩效、奖金激励、人才盘点、ER/PIP
- 外企英文矩阵适配

Generic Commercial HRBP, pure retail HRBP, pure COE/SSC, and pure manufacturing HR should be downgraded unless client facts prove they are relevant.
